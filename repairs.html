FAQ

Questions about repairs

Search or browse our most common repair and service questions.

Where does the repair happen?
Our campus mechanics come to your dock or hostel block — you don't need to bring the bike anywhere. Just flag it in the app and we'll find it.
Are parts included in the price?
Yes. For puncture repair and brake tuning, standard parts are included in the flat rate. For full service, all consumables are included. No surprise add-ons.
What if the repair costs more than expected?
We'll contact you before proceeding if additional parts are needed beyond what's covered. You'll always approve costs before we proceed — no surprises.
Can I use my repair credit from a rental plan?
Yes — Student plan holders get 1 free repair credit per month (puncture or brake), and Explorer/Group plan holders get roadside repair credits included. Credits apply automatically at checkout.
What if I damage a PedalHub bike during my rental?
Damage is assessed at actual repair cost and capped at your plan's insurance limit. Explorer and Group riders have insurance included. Student riders can add damage cover for ₹5/day.
